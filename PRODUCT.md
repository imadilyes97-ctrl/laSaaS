# LinkFlow - PRODUCT.md

## Register
**Product** — SaaS dashboard with marketing landing page. Primary register is product (app UI, admin, dashboard), with a brand surface at `/`.

## Users & Purpose

### Target Users
- Small to medium e-commerce business owners in Algeria/Francophone Africa
- Non-technical merchants who sell via Facebook/Instagram
- Entrepreneurs wanting 24/7 sales automation without hiring staff

### User Context
- Mobile-first but manage from desktop
- French-speaking (Darija/French mix)
- Need simplicity — configurable in minutes, not hours
- Price-sensitive but value-quality

### Job To Be Done
"Automate my customer conversations and order-taking so I can focus on growing my business instead of answering the same questions all day."

### Emotions To Evoke
- **Trust** — reliable, professional, secure
- **Delight** — polished, modern, feels premium
- **Simplicity** — "it just works", no complexity
- **Ambition** — helps them scale

## Brand & Personality
- **Tone**: Direct, warm, confident. French but not overly formal.
- **3 words**: Bold, Warm, Precise
- **Anti-references**: 
  - Generic SaaS templates (shadcn default, corporate blue)
  - Cyber/cyan dark mode (current — too generic tech)
  - Flat, boring dashboards
  - Overuse of Inter font, purple gradients, glassmorphism cards

## Strategic Design Principles
1. **Opacity layers over glass** — depth through dark surfaces, not blur
2. **Bold warmth** — coral/orange primary on dark, not cold blue
3. **Motion with purpose** — every animation answers "why"
4. **Editorial moments** — break the dashboard grid with full-bleed sections
5. **Content is the UI** — strong typography hierarchy, minimal chrome
